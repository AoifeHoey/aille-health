This policy explains what personal data Áille Health Clinic collects through this website, why we collect it, and your rights. We keep it as short and plain as we can.

Who we are

Áille Health Clinic ([legal name of the business or sole trader]), [business address], Co. Wexford, Ireland, is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at info@aillehealth.ie or +353 85 748 6575.

What we collect

  • Your login. Your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see them.
  • Your trade application. Your name, clinic name, phone number, qualification, professional-body membership number (optional), delivery address, and the certificate files you upload.
  • Your orders. The products you order, your delivery address, phone number and email. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Enquiries. What you send us through the enquiry form or by email.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Checking you're a qualified practitioner. Many of our products are supplied only to qualified practitioners. We review your certificate before approving your account (legitimate interests, and steps you ask us to take before entering a contract).
  • Processing and delivering your orders (performance of a contract).
  • Keeping accounts and tax records (legal obligation).
  • Replying to enquiries and emailing you about your account and orders (legitimate interests). We don't send marketing emails without your consent.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use a small number of trusted service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Netlify: website hosting, forms, and secure storage of applications and certificates.
  • Google Firebase: account login.
  • Stripe: card payments and fraud prevention.
  • Resend: sending account and order emails.
  • Our courier ([e.g. An Post / DPD]): your name, address and phone number, to deliver your order.
  • Our accountant, where needed for our accounts.

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it's protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Certificates and application details: while your trade account is open, and for [12 months] after it closes. For applications we don't approve, [6 months].
  • Order and payment records: 6 years, as required by Irish tax law.
  • Enquiries: up to [2 years].

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another organisation. Email info@aillehealth.ie and we'll respond within one month. Some records, such as invoices, must be kept by law even if you ask us to delete them.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. We only use storage that's strictly necessary for the site to work:

  • Login session (Google Firebase): keeps you signed in.
  • Basket: remembers what's in your basket on this device.
  • Checkout (Stripe): cookies set on Stripe's payment page to process your payment securely and prevent fraud.

Because these are strictly necessary, we don't need to ask for consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings, but you'll be signed out and your basket will be emptied.

Security

The site uses HTTPS throughout. Certificates can only be viewed by Áille staff after they log in, and prices and orders are only shown to the account they belong to.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
