Delivery times

Orders are usually dispatched within [1–2 working days] and delivered 2–5 working days after dispatch. We email you when your order is on its way, with tracking where available. Delivery times aren't guaranteed, especially around bank holidays.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Please check your order when it arrives. If anything is damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, email info@aillehealth.ie within [7 days] of delivery, with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full, including any postage.

Returns

  • Because our products are herbal, nutritional or sterile clinical supplies, we can't accept returns of items that have been opened, or whose seals or sterile packaging have been broken. This is for health and hygiene reasons.
  • If you've changed your mind, unopened items in resaleable condition can be returned within [14 days] of delivery. Please email us first. Return postage is paid by you unless the item was faulty or sent in error.
  • If you're buying as a consumer, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of delivery. This right doesn't apply to sealed goods that were unsealed after delivery and can't be returned for health or hygiene reasons.

Refunds

Refunds are made to the card you paid with, within 14 days of us agreeing the refund or receiving the returned items.
